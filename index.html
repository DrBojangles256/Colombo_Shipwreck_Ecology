Interpretation & limitations

Fish composition differed between habitats; diversity and richness were similar. Snappers, cardinalfishes and fusiliers were more abundant on wrecks.

Raw totals (2,169 on wrecks; 749 on reefs) reflect unequal sampling effort and should not be compared as densities. Short-term, six-site observations do not establish increased fish production or causation.

PERMANOVA: family p = 0.022; genus p = 0.010. Density bars alone do not demonstrate a statistically significant density difference.
